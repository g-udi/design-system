# Sample portraits

These photos are Storybook fixture assets, served at `/avatars/<men|women>/<n>.jpg`
(for example `/avatars/women/44.jpg`). They were downloaded once, on 2026-10-02, from the
fixed portrait URLs of [Random User](https://randomuser.me/) (`/api/portraits/<men|women>/<n>.jpg`),
a free generator of sample user data, so stories and copied examples show real faces without
loading anything from the network.

They are demo data only: the names, roles and handles shown beside them are fictional and do
not identify the people in the photographs. They are not part of the published package;
applications supply their own avatar URLs. See `.storybook/fixtures/PORTRAITS.md` for the
identities used across examples.
